Three directions for a portfolio people play on their phone. Each scene below is live: touch it. Then press «قطعی» to feel the moment the story turns on.

جرقه

The Spark · fly through the night

You are a small light flying over a city at night. Every building you pass lights up, and each lit window stands for real people Mahdi's products reached. Then the internet goes out.

The moment: the blackout is played, not watched. The city goes black, the music stops, and you keep flying with only your own glow.

Control
Hold to rise, let go to glide. One thumb.
Story
  1. One laptop glowing on a rooftop in Mashhad.
  2. Shaparo, Chain of Legends, Pargar: each chapter lights more of the city.
  3. Blackout. You fly on in the dark; some towers never light again.
  4. Lights return one by one: ORO, then a crane for ORO-Accountant.
  5. Your spark lands on an empty rooftop and stays. «جای تو اینجاست.»
Why it works
The most emotional of the three, and it records beautifully as a Reel.
Risk
Flight has to feel good, which takes tuning. Two minutes is the attention ceiling.
Build effort
Medium

بساز

Build It · stack the tower

The tap-to-stack game everyone already knows. Each floor is a real milestone, and the tower turns into a lit building as it grows.

The moment: the screen shakes, the lights cut, and floors you built fall away. Then the next block slides in anyway. «ولی ادامه دادیم.»

Control
Tap when the block sits on the one below.
Story
  1. Dizeh, Shaparo, Chain of Legends, Pargar: one floor per milestone.
  2. Blackout: the top floors fall (Eima).
  3. Keep stacking in the dark; the lights come back floor by floor.
  4. The last block is empty. «بلوک بعدی مال توئه.»
Why it works
Easiest to play and the most replayable. People compare heights and send it on.
Risk
The story lives in labels between taps. Players may chase height and skip the words.
Build effort
Low

سیاره

Tiny Planet · spin seven years

Seven years as a small planet you spin with your finger. Each project is a building on it. Tap one to switch its light on and read what happened there.

The moment: one tower never lights up. Tap it and Mahdi tells you why he shut it down himself.

Control
Drag to spin, tap a building to open it.
Story
  1. A dark planet. Only the ORO lighthouse is turning.
  2. Find the shop, the castle, the trading tower, the arcade, Chid's house.
  3. Eima stays dark: the honest one.
  4. Find all eight and an empty plot appears. «یه زمین خالی مونده. مال توئه.»
Why it works
The most beautiful at rest and needs no skill. It works pinned on the page as a place people come back to.
Risk
Least game-like. It's exploration, so there's less reason to replay.
Build effort
Medium
