## Rules for every post (from the research)

**Format**
- 35–60-second Reel: your face plus real proof on screen (split screen with the workflow, dashboard or tool).
- Hook as on-screen text in the first frame. Persian burned-in subtitles (commenters keep asking for them).
- Lead with the result, not the setup ("The cheapest flight with one Telegram message", not "let's build a bot").

**Feasibility line up front.** Every tool post answers in the first seconds: free or paid? Works in Iran / needs a VPN? Needs a foreign card? Persian support?

**No gating.** Full steps go in the caption. The niche's "comment WORD" funnel is breaking: commenters keep writing "my link never came", and Instagram is throttling their DMs.
- Default call to action: **"send this to someone who…"** (DM shares are the strongest reach signal).
- Use a keyword only for a heavier asset (a template), delivered by a Telegram/Bale bot, not Instagram DMs.

**Hook patterns to use honestly**
- A: concrete result in one line (only results you actually got, with time and cost)
- B: contrarian reframe
- C: a question that names the viewer's situation
- D: an owner-pain story

Twenty Persian hook templates are in section 6.3 of the report.

**Avoid:** "secret codes", "10x", "you shouldn't ask ChatGPT this" · dollar-income site lists · AI entertainment and face swaps · giveaways · hidden prices · unlabeled sponsored tool posts.

## Context that shapes these ideas (October 2026)

**From the Instagram research** (25 Persian accounts, 139 Reels, 36 offers)
- **The gap nobody owns:** "I built it, here's the honest result", with time, cost, what broke, whether it works in Iran, and full steps.
- Small accounts broke out with builder or relatable content for a specific person in a specific situation, never with generic tool lists.
- Owner and manager pain stories get the best comments (real owners arguing).
- Money content gets millions of views and the most distrust ("it's a lie").
- **What sells in the niche:** courses through Telegram (3–15M toman), consulting, sponsorships. Prices are usually hidden.

**Iran**
- International internet was cut from 8 January to 26 May 2026. Access is back only partly: connections are slow, social media is still blocked, and VPNs are less reliable and more expensive.
- An estimated 500,000+ Instagram-based shops (about 1 million jobs) lost 50–90% of revenue during the blackout. Many sellers moved to domestic apps (Rubika, Bale), which have outages and high ad prices.
- Inflation is roughly 70–90%, the rial is at record lows, and more than 1 million jobs have been lost. A growing number of skilled people work remotely for companies in the UAE, EU or Canada ("remote migration").
- Local AI options (domestic Persian chatbots, a national AI platform) exist because global tools are hard to reach.

**AI and tools**
- New frontier models came out in September (GPT-6 family, Claude Opus 5.5), with prices varying about 119x between models. Cheap and open-weight models keep improving.
- **Vibe coding**: building apps by describing them to AI. Around 63% of its users are non-developers (Lovable, Bolt and similar tools).
- **AI agents** that complete multi-step tasks with a human checking the result are replacing simple chatbots. n8n's AI Agent node has created a wave of "build and sell AI agents" courses.
- **Offline AI**: small open models (Qwen3, Gemma 3, Phi-4-mini) run on a normal laptop with no internet, using Ollama or LM Studio.

**Instagram**
- The strongest signals for reaching non-followers are **DM sends (shares)**, watch time, and likes per reach. A high skip rate in the first 3 seconds stops distribution.
- Original content is distributed much more than reposts. Reels up to 3 minutes can still be recommended to non-followers.

**Note on sensitive topics:** Ideas that touch the shutdown, the economy, emigration or identity are written as practical business and career advice, not political commentary. The research found these topics split comment sections sharply. Decide your own comfort level with each one.
