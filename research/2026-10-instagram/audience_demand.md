**Pattern:** distrust peaks under (a) money promises, (b) vague "wealth" diagrams and (c) gated freebies. It is lowest under concrete builds (Grok Bot, the flight bot, the CV analyser), where the complaint is "how do I get it?" instead of "is this real?".

## 5. Themes ranked (rough frequency × strength)

1. **Access and practicality: "How do I get it, does it work in Iran, is it free?"** Seen most often, across AI, automation and career content.
2. **Trust: "Is this real or a sales funnel?"** Strongest in money and freelancing content.
3. **Beginner clarity: "Explain simply, step by step, in Persian, with subtitles."**
4. **Career direction: "What should a 20–25-year-old do now?"** Plus the stay-or-leave tension.
5. **Owner operations: staff, systems, "would it work in my business?"**

## 6. Under-served demand

- **A follow-through layer.** People want to own the tool after the Reel (a working bot, a template, setup steps) and the keyword-DM funnel often fails them. A creator who gives the steps openly (aidaasoufi's dashboard caption is the only example found) stands out.
- **Honest Iran-specific feasibility.** Free or paid? Works without VPN? Needs a foreign card? Nobody answers these up front.
- **Practical career paths that don't assume emigration.** The leave-or-stay split is visible and nobody addresses it calmly.
- **Concrete operations content for small owners.** When it's concrete, owners engage and argue (restaurant, employee motivation, ERP vs CRM), but most business content is vague "pillars" and diagrams.
