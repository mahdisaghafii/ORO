# Instagram market research: Persian tech, AI, money and business niche

October 2026. Read-only research from the brief owner's logged-in account.

**Data files:** `accounts.csv` (25 Persian accounts, plus English benchmarks in `discovery_log.md`), `viral_reels.csv` (139 Reels: 126 Persian, 13 English), `offers.csv` (36 offers), `audience_demand.md`, `bio_links.txt`, `reels_grid_raw.txt`.

**How to read the numbers:** views are as shown on each Reels grid on 2 October 2026. "×median" means views divided by the account's median over its latest ~30 non-pinned Reels. Pinned or old Reels compared with a recent median can show inflated multiples, and those are flagged where it matters.

---

## 1. Executive summary: 10 findings

1. **Normal performance varies 400×.** Medians in this niche run from about 2.4K (tamassoki.web) to about 950K (pezhman.husseini), and follower count predicts it poorly. alisiahkolah has 112K followers and a ~4.6K median; linkojob has 79K followers and a ~3.3K median. Large audiences in this niche are often dormant or bought. Judge any page by its median, not its followers.
2. **The biggest Persian hits are entertainment wearing an AI label.** AI meme edits, face-swap parodies and "where is [internet celebrity] now?" nostalgia get 1–5.5M views. They build reach but not trust or buyers.
3. **Within useful content, one formula wins: a concrete result in one line, then "comment WORD".** Examples: "۱۳ سایت درآمد دلاری", "بازیابی فایل بدون هیچ هزینه", "با یک عکس تبلیغ بساز", "این برنامه ورزشی". That pattern produced most 300K–6M educational hits.
4. **The keyword-comment funnel is universal and starting to break.** 59 of 96 analysed Reels with a visible call to action use "comment WORD"; 33 of 112 have more comments than likes. Comment sections fill with "where is my link?", "I sent it and nothing came", and "Instagram blocked our DMs, go to Telegram".
5. **Small and new accounts break out with builder or relatable content, not tool lists.** mahdiss_ai got 15.5K followers from 3 Reels (111K–296K, all Claude explainers for job seekers). hana_techh (7.8K followers) got 151K with a researcher meme. kianipour.ir (11K) got 73K with "stop chatting with ChatGPT, build the employee". sakhtar.system (14.5K, an ERP company) gets 34–134K with manager-pain motion graphics.
6. **Manager and owner pain told as a story is the best business-content format.** Fooladvand's "we got employees addicted to working" got 197K on a 49K median; miladfallah's restaurant lesson got 324K. Owners argue in the comments, which is a strong demand signal.
7. **What people actually ask for is follow-through and feasibility.** "How do I get it?", "Is it free?", "Does it work in Iran?", "Explain step by step". Nobody answers these up front.
8. **Distrust is concentrated in money content.** The "درآمد دلاری" search results are polluted with betting, airdrop and exchange pages. Money hooks still get millions of views, but the comments say "it's a lie" and "why do you care about our income?".
9. **Money comes from courses sold through Telegram, sponsorships and consulting.** Prices seen: 3.1M–15M toman per recorded course (moeinrabanian), $49 for 30-minute consulting (mahbodpour), plus cohort business programs, webinars and raffles. One Persian AI platform (Noghteh) sponsors several creators at once. Persian AI platforms are buying distribution through creators.
10. **Nobody owns "honest builder".** Automation pages either talk like consultants (Fooladvand), sell a service (zimmerhq, tamassoki) or give away gated freebies (mahbodpour). Nobody consistently shows a real build with time, cost, what broke, Iran-feasibility and full steps in the caption. That is your space.

**C. Question that names the viewer's situation**

**F. Number list**

- «۵ کامند کاربردی در ChatGPT» ("5 useful ChatGPT commands"). mahbodpour, 1.3M.
- «۹۰٪ از مهاجرین...» ("90% of migrants..."). job_mohajer, 467K.

### 2.3 Formats and lengths that win
- **Median length of opened Reels: 45 seconds.** 20 were 30s or under, 27 were 31–60s, 14 were over 60s. Long story Reels (miladfallah 101s, pezhman 109s, job_mohajer 146s) can still go viral when the story holds.
- **The most common winning format is a talking head plus proof on screen**: a split screen (screenshot or tool above, face below), a title card, or a screen recording. Pure talking heads without proof are rare among hits.
- **Faceless motion graphics work for B2B** (sakhtar.system, zimmerhq).
- **9-second "text over selfie" Reels with the value in the caption** (kiatechacademy) get high views but shallow trust.
- **Hands drawing on paper** (job_mohajer, linkojob) is a low-cost format that performs.

### 2.4 What small accounts did to go viral
- **mahdiss_ai** (3 posts, 15.5K followers): Claude explainers for a concrete use (CV, interview), on-screen question text, no keyword CTA. Comments asked for steps and subtitles.
- **hana_techh** (7.8K): a relatable meme for a specific identity (researchers/students), with tag-a-friend.
- **shahoosh_ai** (6.4K): an AI-avatar presenter plus the Iran-access pain, or a shocking true story.
- **kianipour.ir** (11K): a contrarian reframe, plus a real local bot (Divar).
- **sakhtar.system** (14.5K): manager scenarios ("one order, three departments asking") plus a client case study ("21 days").

The common thread is a specific person in a specific situation, with something concrete. None of these breakouts came from a generic tool list.

### 2.5 Shares vs saves vs comments (inferred from likes, comments and comment content)
- **Shared (sent to friends):** humour and relatable posts (memes, sibling skits, "send this to the friend who uses ChatGPT all the time"), nostalgia, and identity ("مسیر یک جوان ۲۵ ساله ایرانی").
- **Saved:** lists of tools, commands and prompts, plus LinkedIn/CV guides. Likes are high but comments are low unless there is a keyword.
- **Comments:** anything gated behind "comment WORD" (comments often exceed likes), plus controversial owner stories (real debate).

### 3.3 The most common funnels
1. **Reel → "comment WORD" → auto-DM bot → Telegram channel → free lesson/webinar → paid course.** (moeinrabanian, webmastermentor, farzan_eslami, digitalmentor)
2. **Reel (owner pain) → comment a number → free live webinar → cohort program/coaching.** (miladfallah)
3. **Reel (expertise) → consultation form or booking.** (mahdi.fooladvand1's no-VPN form, mahbodpour's $49 booking, zimmerhq's free consultation)
4. **Reel → bio product links.** (alirezayazdanii's three course pages)
5. **Sponsored Reel → platform link** (Noghteh, Housha, AI aggregators).

Channels used: Telegram dominates; Bale (domestic) appears for Fooladvand, who also runs a form that "works without VPN". WhatsApp appears for linkojob.

### 3.4 Which offers look like they sell vs. look stagnant
- **Look like they sell:** moeinrabanian (many courses, student work reposted, a 912K/133K-median audience); webmastermentor (repeated launches, raffles, DM volume high enough that Instagram blocked DMs); miladfallah (coded cohorts "کد ۱…۶", recurring events and tours); digitalmentor (a wall of student-story highlights); aiolearn (physical branches).
- **Look stagnant or weak:** alisiahkolah (seminar branding but ~5K views); doorkariran (course from 2023 pinned, ~4K views); tamassoki's webinars (~1–3K views on promo Reels); linkojob (book, ~3K median).

### 3.5 Hype and honesty
- About a quarter of opened money and AI Reels make an explicit result or income claim ("همون روز اول به شما درآمد دلاری میدن", "۱۰ برابر قوی‌تر", "در چند ثانیه تیزر بساز", "به بالای ۹۰،۰۰۰ نفر روشم رو یاد دادم").
- **The audience pushes back:** "it's a lie", "dream on, without money?", "10x the cost", "why do you care about our income?". Others leak the gated prompt in the comments.
- Even so, hype Reels still get the views. Honesty doesn't win reach by default; it wins the people who stay and buy.

## 4. Audience demand (details in `audience_demand.md`)
- **Top questions:** how to actually get or use it; free or paid; does it work in Iran; explain it simply in Persian; what should a 20–25-year-old do.

## 5. The gap for you

**Your brand:** practical, anti-hype, builder, product/data/automation background, diagnosis plus reality check.

**Viral patterns you can use honestly**
- **Concrete result in one line** (pattern A), but only results you actually got, with the time and cost.
- **Contrarian reframe** (pattern B). This is already your "Reality Check" series.
- **Situation question** (pattern C), aimed at 18–27-year-olds and small owners.
- **Owner pain story** (pattern D) from your real product and team experience.
- **Split screen with proof** (your face plus a real dashboard, workflow or screen) as the default format.
- **Relatable meme for your niche** (builders, PMs, devs, owners) as a cheap reach format, used occasionally.

**Patterns to avoid**
- "Secret codes / 10x / you shouldn't ask ChatGPT this".
- Dollar-income lists.
- AI entertainment.
- Giveaways of accounts or "Claude Max links".
- Gating everything behind a keyword.
- Hidden prices.

**Space nobody owns**
1. **"I built it, here's the honest result"**, with time, cost, what broke, whether it works in Iran, and full steps in the caption.
2. **Feasibility first.** Every tool post answers free/paid, VPN, card, Persian support in the first seconds.
3. **Systems for small owners with numbers**: pricing under inflation, cost cutting, a customer list you own, one dashboard. The interest is visible (sakhtar, fooladvand), but nobody shows the actual system.
4. **Career reality without the emigration default**: what to learn, how to test a field in 2 weeks, remote work with honest barriers.

### 6.4 Format and length to start with
- **Default:** 35–60-second Reel, with your face plus real proof (a split screen of the workflow, dashboard or screen), Persian burned-in subtitles (requested in comments), and the hook as on-screen text in the first frame.
- **Caption:** full steps and feasibility notes (free/paid, VPN, card, Persian), with no gate on the core value.
- **Secondary formats:** carousels for feasibility tables and checklists (save-worthy), and an occasional niche meme for reach.
- **Call to action:** "send this to…" for shares. Use one keyword CTA only for a heavier asset (a template), and make sure delivery works, ideally through a Telegram/Bale bot rather than DMs.

## 7. Risks
- **Saturated:** ChatGPT prompt drops, "secret codes", AI video/face/voice tricks, dollar-income site lists, AI aggregator sponsorships, n8n freebie giveaways.
- **Niche-wide trust problem:** money keywords are polluted by betting, airdrop and exchange pages. Commenters already assume funnels and lies. Keep money content analytical and never promise income.
- **Funnel fragility:** Instagram is already throttling DMs for big keyword funnels. Don't build your funnel on Instagram DMs alone.
- **Platform risk:** Instagram is blocked in Iran and the 2026 shutdown cut access for months. Keep an owned channel (Telegram/Bale list, email, website) from day one, and practise what H5-6 preaches.
- **Sponsorship pull:** Persian AI platforms are actively buying creator posts. Sponsored tool posts would erode an anti-hype brand unless clearly labelled and actually tested.
- **Sensitive topics:** emigration, sanctions and identity spark strong splits in the comments. Frame them as practical career and business decisions.

## 8. Method and limits
- **Sample:**
  - 37 keywords searched (account results, 5 per keyword) plus "similar accounts" chains; ~95 accounts checked; 25 Persian accounts analysed (10 big, 12 mid, 3 small).
  - Reels grid: ~30 latest Reels per account (fewer for some), views as shown.
  - 139 Reels in `viral_reels.csv`: 61 opened (length, caption, visible comments, first-frame screenshot); the rest from post metadata only (likes, comments, date, caption), marked "(not viewed)".
- **Small tier is under target** (3 vs 8–10). Instagram search surfaces big accounts first, and several small candidates had no Reels or no outliers.
- **English benchmark:** profile check for 9 creators plus 13 Reels from English keyword search (likes only). Their Reels grids didn't load reliably.
- **What I couldn't see:**
  - Spoken first lines (no audio). hook_text uses on-screen text or the caption's first line.
  - DM-send and save counts.
  - Comments beyond the first ~15 loaded.
  - Highlights content (titles only, by rule).
  - Most Iranian-hosted course sites, which timed out from the research environment, so prices are partial.
  - Phase 5's extra 10–15 comment sections, because the browser connection dropped.
- **Personalisation bias:** search results, "similar accounts" and comment ordering are personalised to the researcher's account. The sample leans toward tech/AI creators the account already sees.
- **Dates:** derived from post metadata. Views are a snapshot from 2 October 2026.
- **Rules followed:** no likes, follows, comments, saves, DMs, Story or Highlight views, sign-ups or purchases. Telegram was read only through public `t.me/s/` previews. No Instagram warnings were encountered.
