# Audience demand in the saved posts

Read-only. Source: the visible comments (about 10–20 per post) on the 21 posts saved on `@oro_platform`, read on 2026-10-13. Counts are rough and cover only what the page showed. Comments that are only the keyword ("لینک", "FDE", "73") are counted separately, because they show the gate working, not what people want.

## 1. The gate is the main thing people type

- 14 of 21 captions ask for a keyword comment (`saved_posts.csv`, `cta`). Three more posts show keyword comments although the caption does not ask (posts 3, 4 and a "Code" request on post 8).
- On posts 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 13, 16, 17 and 18 nearly every visible comment is the keyword. Posts 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 13, 16 and 17 have more comments than likes.
- So comment counts on gated posts say little about interest in the topic. They measure how many people were willing to type one word.

## 3. What this says

1. **Feasibility comes before everything.** The one post that stated a build time, an output length and token cost (post 7) got a comment thanking it for not calling the tool free. The questions under it were all about access and price in Iran.
2. **People ask "how" far more than they argue.** The how-to requests are unanswered in the comments in view.
3. **Distrust is aimed at the result and at the gate, not at the person.** "Fake code" and "AI slop" target the demo. "Commented but never shared" targets the funnel.
4. **Price questions on posts that hide prices.** Post 7 hides the cost of the Pro account and gets four price questions. Post 19 shows its price (899,000 toman per month) and its questions are about how it works, not whether it is too expensive.

## 4. Under-served (compared with `../2026-10-instagram/audience_demand.md`)

- A tool post that states Iran access (cost, VPN/IP, ban risk) in the video itself.
- Full steps in the caption, with no keyword gate.
- A real build with time, cost and what went wrong.
- A mobile or small-business version of the "AI-built website" demo: nobody in this set shows one.

## 5. Limits

- Only the first screenful of comments is visible, and Instagram sorts them by its own ranking, so quiet but common requests may be missing.
- Likes on the comments are shown only when above zero.
- Comments in English were read as written. Persian comments are quoted from the page in the CSV where short, otherwise paraphrased here.
